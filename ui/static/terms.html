Who this is for

Clinical trial coordinators, research nurses, clinicians, and researchers evaluating whether compiled eligibility logic is useful in their workflow.

It is not for patients or carers. If you or someone you care for is looking for a trial, please work through your treating oncologist, or start at ClinicalTrials.gov. Eligibility is a clinical judgement that this tool cannot make, and a result here does not mean a trial will accept or reject you.

What the tool actually does, and where it stops

  • It compiles trial eligibility criteria into executable predicates and evaluates them deterministically. Roughly 60% of criteria compile; the rest remain free text and are not evaluated at all. A trial shown as "eligible" has only been checked against the criteria that compiled.
  • Extraction from your note is automated and imperfect. A missed or misread value produces a wrong verdict. Every verdict shows the exact sentence it was computed from so you can check it — please do.
  • The trial corpus is a fixed snapshot of ClinicalTrials.gov, not a live feed. Recruiting status goes stale quickly; sites listed as recruiting are often closed. Confirm status with the trial site.
  • Scope is non-small cell lung cancer only, 500 trials, English. Results for anything else are not meaningful.
  • Computed dates are exact calendar arithmetic over a date stated in your note. If that anchor date is absent or wrong, the computed date is meaningless — which is why the anchor is always displayed.
