# Notice

**This is not medical advice and not a medical device.**

No regulator has reviewed or cleared this software. It makes no diagnosis and
no treatment recommendation. It is a research and screening-support tool for
qualified professionals, and every output must be independently verified before
it informs any decision about a person's care.

**It is not for patients.** Eligibility is a clinical judgement this software
cannot make. If you or someone you care for is looking for a trial, work through
your treating oncologist, or start at <https://clinicaltrials.gov>.

**Known limits, stated plainly.**

- **39.7% of eligibility criteria never compile** and are not evaluated at all.
  A trial shown as "eligible" has only been checked against the criteria that
  did compile.
- Extraction from a note is automated and imperfect. Every verdict displays the
  sentence it was computed from so it can be checked — please check it.
- The trial corpus is a **fixed snapshot**, not a live feed. Recruiting status
  goes stale quickly; sites listed as recruiting are often closed.
- Scope is **non-small cell lung cancer only**, 500 trials, English.
- Computed dates are exact calendar arithmetic over a date stated in the note.
  If that anchor is absent or wrong, the computed date is meaningless, which is
  why the anchor is always shown.

**Do not submit identifiable patient data** to the hosted demo. It runs on
ordinary commercial hosting with no Business Associate Agreement and is not
configured for HIPAA-regulated protected health information. See
<https://trial-eligibility-compiler.vercel.app/privacy.html>.
